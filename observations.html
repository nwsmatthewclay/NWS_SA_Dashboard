Wind chill is calculated from co-timed temperature and wind observations where temperature is 50°F or colder and wind is at least 3 mph. Heat index is calculated from temperature and dewpoint-derived relative humidity where temperature is at least 80°F and relative humidity is at least 40%. Both use standard NWS/NOAA equations. Only stations with the required observations are included. Ice-accretion products retain hourly QC and flat/radial measurements.
