How This Dashboard Works

A quick guide to finding active hazards, controlling what appears on the map, and creating NWS graphics.

🗺️ Click a hazard

Click any highlighted zone or warning polygon on the map to open its details, including timing, affected area, impacts, and safety information.

🖼️ Create an image

From a hazard popup, choose Create Image for This Hazard. The dashboard automatically fits the map to that product and creates the graphic — no dragging or map selection required.

🎛️ Zone & polygon controls

Zone Warnings controls zone, county, and fire-weather products. Polygon Warnings controls storm-based warning polygons. Turn either on or off independently.

📋 Legend

Click a hazard type in the legend to hide or show that product type on the map. This also controls what is included when you use the map export.

📍 Office & state views

Use the selector at the top to view an individual NWS Weather Forecast Office or switch to a state-wide view.

⏱️ Times & updates

The dashboard automatically refreshes active hazards. Use the time-zone selector to change how product times are displayed.

Choose Export Type

Choose what to export, then choose how tightly the map should be framed.
